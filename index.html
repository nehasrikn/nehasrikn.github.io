Hi there 👋! I'm a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, where I work with Profs. Rachel Rudinger and Jordan Boyd-Graber. I'm a member of the Computational Linguistics and Information Processing (CLIP) Lab in UMIACS.

Before coming to Maryland, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Lyft on the Applied Machine Learning team. Before that, I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Austin, where I worked with Jessy Li on elaboration during text simplification.

My research interests lie in natural language processing, particularly in natural language understanding, including problems such as commonsense reasoning, textual inference, and pragmatics.
